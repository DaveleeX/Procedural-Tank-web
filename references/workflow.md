# Milestone workflow

## M0 — Intake and evidence

Preserve originals. List every visible element, its image footprint, priority, complexity, occlusion, and confidence. Note whether multiple images show the same state and whether dimensions are known.

Acceptance: no unlisted major visible object; uncertainties are written rather than silently guessed.

## M1 — Camera and envelope

Build neutral-color primary masses only. Calibrate perspective/orthographic projection, azimuth, elevation, target, FOV, image crop, and model scale. Use prominent verticals, roof edges, corner tangencies, and ground contact as anchors.

Acceptance: outer silhouette, major visible faces, ground contact, and image framing align. Freeze camera and envelope parameters.

## M2 — Architectural/product grammar

Add major openings and repeated systems from counted observations. Derive repetitions from arrays or pattern functions. Parameterize bay widths, offsets, floor heights, and corner transitions.

Acceptance: counts, spacing rhythm, major negative spaces, and visible asymmetries match.

## M3 — Signature systems

Build stairs, balconies, rails, awnings, roofs, mechanical clusters, handles, frames, or other identity-bearing structures. Keep each family in a dedicated module.

Acceptance: the subject is recognizable in flat gray without textures or clutter.

## M4 — Complex context objects

Reconstruct machinery, vehicles, furniture, or ornaments individually. Model primary silhouette and articulation first, then supported mechanical detail. Test close-up and scene-camera views.

Acceptance: object type, pose, scale, orientation, and occlusion agree with the original.

## M5 — Secondary detail

Add equipment, signs, pipes, dishes, lamps, bins, debris, cables, and household clutter. Use clusters and controlled variation where exact geometry is unresolved, but preserve observed count and massing.

Acceptance: all inventory rows have a status and evidence; no invented high-salience object remains.

## M6 — Hidden sides and presentation

Use repeated grammar and construction logic for unseen faces. Make hidden-side code easy to replace when new evidence arrives. Apply restrained material groups after geometry passes.

Acceptance: inferred faces are coherent and labeled; materials do not hide geometry mismatch.

## M7 — Production audit

Run, inspect, render, export, re-import, and document. Use the fixed reference view plus canonical views. Prepare final comparison and readiness report.

Acceptance: source and advertised exports open successfully; bounds and orientation are sensible; limitations and manufacturing status are explicit.

## Iteration discipline

For each pass, log hypothesis, parameter(s) changed, fixed camera, render paths, observed improvement/regression, and decision. Modify one mismatch class per pass. When an accepted milestone regresses, restore it before continuing.
